We don't just maintain transformers — we protect the power that drives your success.

What we do

Predictive Maintenance

Early signs of degradation, identified before they become failures: dissolved gas analysis (DGA), furan analysis, degree of polymerization, PCB testing, physical and chemical oil analysis, and thermal condition assessment.

Electrical Testing & Diagnostics

In-depth performance analysis of critical components: winding resistance, turns ratio, insulation resistance, power factor, core excitation, and sweep frequency response analysis (SFRA).

Preventive Maintenance

Care that preserves system integrity and extends transformer life: oil draining and filling, vacuum processing and degassing, dielectric oil dehydration, OLTC inspection and servicing, oil-paper insulation regeneration, and Fuller's Earth oil treatment.

Corrective Maintenance

Fast, precise on-site response when failure or degradation occurs: assembly, installation and startup, disassembly and relocation, oil leak correction, gasket and seal replacement, OLTC synchronization, and supply of spare parts.

OLTC Programs

Predictive and preventive programs dedicated to on-load tap changers: performance analysis, testing, multi-brand adaptation, and scheduled servicing that keeps voltage regulation stable.

Specialized Crews & Solar

Specialized labor and maintenance crews for energy projects across the Americas — the United States, Canada, and Latin America — and services for solar farms, from project support to operation and maintenance.
